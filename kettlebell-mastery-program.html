5-Day Kettlebell Plan — Full Body, Core-Focused

Equipment: 2×8kg, 1×12kg, 1×18kg  |  Format: AM (30 min) + PM (30 min), 5 days/week  |  No Turkish Get-Ups

Every day touches core. AM sessions are strength-biased (heavier bells, compound lifts). PM sessions are core/conditioning-biased (lighter bells, higher reps, shorter rest). Body-part emphasis rotates so nothing gets overtrained across the week.

Notes

  • Load logic: 18kg for the heaviest single-arm hinge/press/row patterns, 12kg for moderate single-arm and rotational work, 2×8kg for double-bell squats/presses/rows and higher-rep core work.
  • Progression: once all sets/reps feel solid with good form for 2 weeks straight, add 1–2 reps per set before adding load.
  • Recovery: if a session feels too much on top of the AM one, drop PM core work to 2 rounds instead of 3 — consistency across all 10 sessions matters more than intensity in any one.
  • Form first on single-arm deadlifts, cleans, and swings — these carry the most injury risk if rushed under fatigue.

Kettlebell Mastery Program

A 4-phase, ~18+ month progression from foundation to elite-level kettlebell performance. Log your sessions in the tables provided.

Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–4)

Goal: Bulletproof technique on the "big 4" — swing, goblet squat, get-up, clean. No load progression until movement quality is consistent.

Weekly Structure (4 sessions/week)

DayFocusWork
MonSwing + Breathing10 sets x 10 hardstyle swings, 45s rest. Anatomical breathing drill 2x5 min
TueGet-Up Technical5–10 total reps (slow, per side), no rush — quality only
ThuGoblet Squat + Clean5x5 goblet squat, 8x3 clean per side
SatGS Intro (Long Cycle basics)5 sets x 5 min light continuous cycling at very light bell

Load guidance: Men: 16kg (start) → 20kg. Women: 8kg → 12kg. Progress only when form is unbroken for full sets.

Key metric to track: Rep quality (video yourself weekly), not weight.

Phase 2: Technical Refinement (Months 4–10)

Goal: Build true GS jerk/long cycle mechanics + strength-endurance via ladders/waves/EDT.

Weekly Structure (5 sessions/week)

DayFocusWork
MonLadders (Strength)Clean & Press ladder: 1-2-3 reps x 5 rungs, per side, 90s between rungs
TueLong Cycle Technical4 sets x 4 min continuous, focus on leg drive + bell parking
WedRest/MobilityWrist, shoulder, hip mobility (20 min)
ThuWaves (Volume)Swing wave: 10-15-20-15-10, 60s rest between waves
SatEDT Block15 min: alternate clean & press / front squat every 90s, max quality reps

Load progression: Increase bell size only after hitting 10 clean minutes of Long Cycle at current weight with unbroken form.

Underused drill to add: Weak-side unilateral volume — add 30% extra reps on your non-dominant side every session.

Phase 3: Specialization (Months 10–18)

Choose ONE lane. Both are provided — pick based on your goal (raw strength vs. competitive GS).

Lane A: Hardstyle Strength (StrongFirst-style)

DayFocusWork
MonHeavy Grind5x5 heavy goblet/front squat, 5x3 heavy get-up
WedBallistic Power10x5 heavy swings, explosive, full reset each rep
FriPress Strength5x5 strict press per side, ladder to a heavy single

Lane B: Sport GS (Competition Prep)

DayFocusWork
MonLong Cycle Sets2 x 10 min continuous at competition bell weight
WedPacing Practice6 x 3 min at exact competition cadence (~1 rep/2-3 sec)
FriJerk Technical100 reps broken into manageable sets, focus on soft knees/rack efficiency

Grip work (both lanes, 2x/week): Thick-handle carries, pinch grip holds, 3x30-60s.

Phase 4: Elite Refinement (18+ Months)

Goal: Race-pace conditioning, contrast training, and recovery discipline.

Weekly Structure (5-6 sessions/week)

DayFocusWork
MonContrast TrainingHeavy grind (3-5 reps) immediately followed by explosive ballistic (8-10 reps), 4 rounds
TueCompetition PacingFull event simulation at target cadence and heart-rate zone
WedActive RecoveryMobility + light technique work only
ThuComplex TrainingBarbell/kettlebell combo — e.g., heavy squat + kettlebell swing superset
FriVolume Long CycleBuild toward full 10-min competition set at heavier bell
SunDeload (every 3–4 weeks)50% volume, technique focus only

Recovery protocols to institutionalize:

  • Planned deload every 3–4 weeks regardless of how you feel
  • Callus/forearm care after every heavy session
  • Wrist and shoulder mobility as non-negotiable daily practice (10 min)

Progression Checkpoints

  • End of Phase 1: Clean, unbroken form on all 4 foundational lifts for full prescribed volume
  • End of Phase 2: 10 continuous minutes of Long Cycle at working weight
  • End of Phase 3: Hit a lane-specific benchmark (heavy single press or full competition simulation)
  • End of Phase 4: Sustained competition-pace performance across a full event distance

Reassess and adjust bell weights/volumes every 4 weeks based on the tracking template above.
